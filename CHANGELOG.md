# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-19
First release.
- Brighter, warmer torch light (adjustable).
- Cooler, softer shadows and warm sunlight.
- Pixelated player-silhouette shadow (tiny entity-only shadow map).
- Atmosphere styles: Dark (horror mood) and Eclipse (solar eclipse mood), with adjustable strength.
- Wavy water: animated surface, flat tinted water, sky reflection and sun/moon glint. Vanilla water texture available as an option.
- Rainy weather: wet blocks, pixelated puddles, light sky reflection.
- No post-processing passes. Built for low-end PCs.
